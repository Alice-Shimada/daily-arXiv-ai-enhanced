Method: 构建核子触发的矢量玻色子模型 $V_{\mathcal B}$，分析其对η→π⁰γγ衰变中γγ和π⁰γ不变质量谱的影响，量化MAMI与JEF实验因不同入射能量导致的运动学与选择窗依赖效应，并计算异常尾部事件数、反冲质子运动学、重建质量偏移及可能影响γd→π⁰ηd和γd→π⁰π⁰d角分布的拓扑结构。

Result: 预测在1200事例中，η→π⁰γγ衰变的运动学禁戒区将出现4–23个$V_{\mathcal B}$诱导事件；MAMI能量下反冲质子极角可达≈−5°，JEF下仅≈−0.2°；相同选择窗下JEF与MAMI有效分支比差异<1%，拓宽窗口则两者均增约4%；两实验均使重建质量均值上移2–7 MeV；识别出若干可能解释γd→π⁰ηd/π⁰π⁰d角分布反常的$V_{\mathcal B}$拓扑。

Conclusion: 核子触发的$V_{\mathcal B}$模型可自然解释η→π⁰γγ分支比的实验分歧，其最干净信号是γγ/π⁰γ谱中的禁戒区尾部事件，且该模型亦可能关联近期双介子光核反应中的角分布异常，值得进一步定量检验。

Abstract: The nucleon-triggered vector-boson scenario $V_{\mathcal B}$ was motivated by a statistically significant discrepancy between the KLOE measurement, $\mathrm{BR}^{η\rightarrowπ^{0}γγ}_{\text{KLOE}} = \left(0.98\pm0.11_{\text{stat}}\pm0.14_{\text{syst}}\right)\times10^{-4}$, and the current world average, $\left(2.55\pm0.22\right)\times10^{-4}$, dominated by MAMI photoproduction data. In this model, the new interaction is activated by external nucleons, which causes deviations from the Standard Model predictions, while leaving leptonic measurements, such as KLOE and BESIII, unaffected. We quantify the residual kinematic and cut-dependent effects that make the MAMI- and JEF-like predictions close but not identical due to their different production-energy regimes. The cleanest discriminator of the model is a kinematically forbidden tail in the $γγ$ and $π^0γ$ spectra, and for $η\rightarrowπ^0γγ$, and we predict 4--23 events in a sample of 1200, depending on the selection window, in these regions. These events correlate with the kinematics of the recoil proton, and the polar angle relative to the on-shell hypothesis can reach $\approx-5^\circ$ or below at MAMI energies, but only $\approx -0.2^\circ$ at JEF. For identical selection windows, the JEF-like effective branching fraction exceeds the MAMI-like prediction by less than $1\%$, while widening the window increases both predictions by up to $\sim4\%$. Both energy regimes produce a similar upward shift of the mean reconstructed mass, $\langle M_{π^0γγ}\rangle-m_η\simeq 2$--$7~\mathrm{MeV}$. Finally, we identify representative $V_{\mathcal B}$-induced topologies that could contribute to recently reported discrepancies in the angular distributions of $γd\toπ^0ηd$ and $γd\toπ^0π^0 d$, leaving their quantitative investigation to future work.
