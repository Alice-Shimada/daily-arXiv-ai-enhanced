### [1] [Anomaly in baryon number](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29135)
*Yutaka Hosotani*

Main category: hep-ph

TL;DR: 本文研究了RS弯曲空间中GUT启发的SO(5)×U(1)×SU(3)规范-希格斯统一模型中的手征反常，特别关注重子数和轻子数流的反常，并通过KK激发模在UV/IR膜上的波函数表达总反常。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 为理解大统一理论（GUT）框架下重子与轻子数守恒的破缺机制，尤其是在额外维模型中手征反常的完整结构及其对膜上物理的影响。

Method: 基于Randall-Sundrum弯曲时空的5维规范-希格斯统一模型，系统计算包含全部Kaluza-Klein费米子激发模的三角图贡献，利用其在UV和IR膜上的波函数值表征总手征反常。

Result: 得到5维重子数流的协变散度在UV和IR膜上具有非零反常项，形式为Tr F_{μν} \tilde F^{μν} 在SU(2)_L与SU(2)_R子群上的差值。

Conclusion: 该模型中重子数手征反常局域于边界膜上，且由左右SU(2)场强张量的拓扑密度差驱动，为探索B/L破缺与额外维物理提供了新视角。

Abstract: Chiral anomalies in baryon and lepton number currents in the GUT-inspired $SO(5) \times U(1) \times SU(3)$ gauge-Higgs unification model in the Randall-Sundrum (RS) warped space are examined. Total anomalies including contributions of all Kaluza-Klein (KK) excited modes of fermions running along internal triangular loops are expressed in terms of the values of wave functions of gauge-boson KK towers at the ultraviolet (UV) and infrared (IR) branes in the RS space. The covariant divergence of 5D baryon number current picks up an anomaly term proportional to ${\rm Tr} \,F_{μν} \tilde F^{μν} |_{SU(2)_L} - {\rm Tr} \, F_{μν} \tilde F^{μν} |_{SU(2)_R}$ at the UV and IR branes where $SU(2)_L \times SU(2)_R$ is a subgroup of $SO(5)$.
